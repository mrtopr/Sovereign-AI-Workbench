MRPL REPORTING PACKAGE
=======================
Industrial operations, inspection, safety and management-reporting documents
prepared for the Sovereign AI Workbench demonstration environment.

The package contains:
• Vessel inspection and technical approval note
• OISD statutory compliance dossier
• Hydrocracker emergency shutdown procedure
• Desalter quality and laboratory log
• Ultrasonic thickness measurement assessment
• Board operational and financial review
• Corrosion-rate technical analysis
• HCU HAZOP / ESD interlock verification report

Document references, personnel names, measurements and management figures are
prepared as scenario data for the demonstration environment.
